About the Repository
What
What is the NMDR?
  • A national digital infrastructure for malaria programme data
  • Hosted on DHIS2 integrated with HMIS, LMIS
  • Developed by NMCP, ICF-SL, WHO, CHAI
Why
Why the NMDR Exists
  • Consolidate fragmented malaria data into one repository
  • Eliminate data silos between systems
  • Strengthen national data governance
Focus
Focus Areas
  • Case management testing treatment reporting
  • Vector control IRS coverage ITN distribution
  • Malaria in pregnancy IPTp ANC attendance
Benefits
Expected Benefits
  • Faster evidence-based programme planning
  • Improved data quality through validation
  • Enhanced transparency and accountability

Application Platform

School-Based Distribution (SBD) App

Comprehensive digital ecosystem for ITN 2026 campaign management, data collection, and distribution tracking across schools in Sierra Leone.

📱 SBD App Features

  • ✓ Multi-role PWA (ICF Collect, ITN Movement, ID Card Generators)
  • ✓ School location cascading selection with CSV backend
  • ✓ Google Sheets/GAS integration for real-time syncing
  • ✓ AI-powered chat agent for school targeting
  • ✓ QR code generation for school identification
  • ✓ Staff ID card generation with bulk upload
  • ✓ Knowledge assessment tools for training validation
  • ✓ ITN distribution movement tracking with DMS logistics
  • ✓ Duplicate blocking and quality assurance features
